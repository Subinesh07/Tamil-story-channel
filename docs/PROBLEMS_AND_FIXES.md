## Checks every video passes before upload
1. Script: Gemini only, logic check (scene-by-scene continuity) with automatic fixes, English words converted to Tamil letters (Tanglish kept), style score ≥ 7 (start, middle, end), no foreign letters, length in range, ending resolves (final parts), not a copy of a specific work.
2. Voice: Leda only (extra key first, main key second); every passage passes the **listening check** (clean Tamil, matches the script); quota = wait, never another voice.
3. Pictures: character cards (age, gender, facial hair) in every prompt; every picture is checked by Gemini against its description AND the narration spoken during it (right objects and places, right people in the right place, five fingers per hand, no merged or extra limbs, no cut-off heads, right ages and facial hair); a picture is never used unchecked or rejected; every picture real and different (no test pattern, blank or duplicate); any failure = wait.
4. Video: no clicks or clipping in the audio (click check), duration in range, subtitles match sentences, audio/video in sync, H.264/AAC format, Tamil letters render.
5. Upload: not already on YouTube (same title); a remake waits until the old video is deleted.
6. Thumbnail: curiosity layout with picture (story picture if the allowance is out).

## 28. Leda spoke English / voice out of sync with subtitles (27 Nov 2026)
**What happened:** Part 2 of "Amma's House" went live with Leda speaking English in one stretch, which pushed
the voice out of sync with the subtitles. Two other videos had passages saved the same way.
**Why:** on 26 Nov, to beat a Gemini overload, the voice step was changed to KEEP a passage without checking it
when Gemini could not listen. Overnight Gemini was only rate-limited (429), so passages were kept unchecked.
On top of that, the yes/no listening check is not reliable on 3–4 minute passages.
**Fix:** (1) a passage is never kept unchecked again - if Gemini cannot listen, the video pauses;
(2) a second check: Gemini writes the passage out word by word in 60-second pieces and plain code compares it
with the script (4+ English words not in the script, or the length off by more than 25%, or under 60% match = re-voice);
(3) affected videos were hidden/deleted and remade after re-listening (`recheck_voice` in the row's fail_reason);
(4) new analytics modes: `private:`, `public:`, `postpone:`, `postponevid:`, `setthumb:`, `clip:`/`listen:` (transcribe audio).
**Update (same evening):** Gemini's word-by-word transcript also failed - it *translated* Leda's English into the
matching Tamil script. **Whisper** (plain speech recognition, `src/langcheck.py`) found it exactly: 3:45-4:02 English
at 76-90%; Leda was reading our own style instructions ("…as written, never translate…"). Whisper now checks every
Leda passage in 5-second steps and the finished video before upload; English anywhere = re-voice / no upload.
Manual check of any video: Actions → "Language check" → file `story_<id>.mp4` (optional zoom "start end").
