Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Tamil Story Uploader is a personal automation tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel.

What it accesses

It uses the YouTube Data API with the youtube.upload permission, granted by the channel owner, to upload videos to that owner's own channel. It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data.

What it stores

The OAuth credentials are kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub settings. No data about viewers or any other person is collected, sold or shared.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
